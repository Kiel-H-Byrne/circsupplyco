STYLE_GUIDE.md: Visual Identity & E-Commerce Aesthetic
1. Core Brand Identity & Concept
Aesthetic Theme: "Next-Gen Futurism meets Quiet Luxury"
For 2025 and 2026, the streetwear landscape bridges the gap between high-tech retro-futurism and refined, minimalist elegance. The brand identity should feel both exclusive and experimental, combining clean e-commerce UI with bold, unapologetic merchandise designs.

Vibe: Sophisticated, energetic, eco-conscious, and tech-forward.

Target Demographic: Gen Z and young Millennials who value sustainable aesthetics, pop culture, and niche streetwear communities.

2. E-Commerce UI/UX (MedusaJS Implementation)
Because the backend utilizes MedusaJS v2, the frontend will leverage Medusa UI—a highly composable React implementation built on Radix Primitives and Tailwind CSS.

Layout: Embrace a clean, grid-based layout that allows product photography to take center stage, minimizing visual distractions.

Interactions: Utilize dynamic image overlays, snappy scroll-triggered animations, and fast transitions to create a feeling of youth and motion.

Color Palette (Web UI):

Backgrounds: Stark monochromatic bases (true black #000000 or crisp white #FFFFFF) to create a "Quiet Luxury" feel.

Accents: Use bold "electric" colors like neon grids or holographic gradients for buttons and interactive elements.

Typography: Mix minimalism with maximalism. Use a clean, modern sans-serif for UI elements and product details, paired with an expressive, custom display font for large hero banners and storytelling sections.

3. Merchandise Design Guidelines
When the autonomous agents generate new t-shirt graphics, they must adhere to the following 2025/2026 streetwear trends:

Graphics & Art Style:

Retro-Futurism: Blend nostalgia (90s/Y2K) with futuristic elements like schematic diagrams, geometric shapes, and digital artifacts.

Eco & Nature: Incorporate stylized natural elements, abstract landscapes, and organic shapes.

Typography on Apparel: Utilize bold, minimalist typography and statement slogans. Messages should be personality-driven, positive, or humorous.

Silhouettes & Apparel Types: Ensure mockups feature oversized streetwear fits, baby tees, and heavy layering.

Brand Patterns: Ensure the brand uses distinct, recognizable patterns across the apparel and the website background. Unique brand patterns are crucial, as 80% of consumers recall brands with strong visual pattern systems.
